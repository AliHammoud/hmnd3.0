Challenge
Keep robotic synthesis reliable while giving operators a real way to intervene when science drifts — without ripping out the automation already in place.
What we did
  • Audited where silent failures hid inside otherwise “correct” robotic runs
  • Designed human-in-the-loop controls and exception surfaces on existing automation
  • Kept bench operators in the decision loop instead of automating agency away
  • Reduced scrap by making recoverable drifts visible early enough to act
