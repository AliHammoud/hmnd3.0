→ 01 · The cost of the wrong question

$38,000.

Three engineers. One PM. One BA. Two months.

A research team was building a test result analysis suite. We were brought in to improve the UX of their report builder.

One week of user research. One finding: the scientists needed to print batch results and scotch-tape them to the lab bench next to the physical sample.

The fix was a print button.

Two months and $38,000 to ask the wrong question.

→ 03 · Approach

How we work

  1. → 01

    Preserve human agency

    Preserving human agency in every AI-assisted workflow we design. Losing agency to automation is the disaster that can’t be averted after the fact.

  2. → 02

    Embed domain scientists

    In-house pharmacologists and scientists, embedded on every engagement — we co-architect solutions with your domain experts, not around them.

  3. → 03

    Fix the question first

    The problem is never the engineers. It’s that the question being asked is wrong. We identify it, reframe it, and translate it into something engineering can actually build.

  4. → 04

    Stay as translation layer

    We don’t deliver a report and disappear. We stay — a permanent bridge between what your science demands and what your engineering can deliver.
